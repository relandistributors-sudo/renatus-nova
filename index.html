Why Nutrition & Lifestyle Matter

Nutrition provides bioactive compounds and nutrients that participate in normal cellular processes. Nutrigenomics studies how nutrients and dietary components interact with biological pathways and gene expression.

Cellular Support

Nutrients and phytonutrients participate in normal cell membrane and antioxidant-related processes.

Pathway Interaction

Dietary components can influence biological pathways that relate to energy, recovery and balance.

Individual Variation

Responses to nutrition and lifestyle differ from person to person based on many factors.

Holistic Context

Supplements are best understood as one part of a broader nutrition and lifestyle approach.

Genes influence susceptibility and biological responses, but genes are not destiny. Nutrition and lifestyle should be considered as part of a broader, individualized health approach.
